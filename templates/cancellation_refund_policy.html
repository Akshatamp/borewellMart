Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel their service bookings under the following conditions:

Customer Cancellations
  • Full Refund: Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the scheduled service time
  • 50% Refund: Cancellations made between 12-24 hours before the scheduled service time
  • No Refund: Cancellations made less than 12 hours before the scheduled service time
  • Emergency Cancellations: In case of genuine emergencies, customers may contact support for consideration
Service Provider Cancellations
  • If a service provider cancels a booking, the customer is entitled to a full refund
  • Service providers must provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations
  • In case of provider cancellation, BorewellMart will assist in finding an alternative provider

2. How to Cancel a Booking

  • Log in to your BorewellMart account
  • Navigate to your Dashboard
  • Select the booking you wish to cancel
  • Click on "Cancel Booking" and provide a reason
  • Confirm the cancellation
  • You will receive a confirmation email with refund details

3. Refund Policy

Refunds are processed according to the following guidelines:

Refund Processing Time
  • Refund Initiation: Refunds are initiated within 5-7 business days after cancellation approval
  • Payment Gateway Processing: Razorpay typically processes refunds within 5-10 business days
  • Total Time: Complete refund may take 10-15 business days to reflect in your account
Refund Method
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method used for the booking
  • If the original payment method is no longer available, refunds will be processed to a bank account via NEFT
  • Customer must provide valid bank details for NEFT refunds

4. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Bookings cancelled less than 12 hours before service time
  • No-show cases where the customer does not appear at the scheduled time
  • Services that have been partially or fully completed
  • Cancellations due to customer-provided incorrect information
  • Promotional or discounted bookings with special no-refund terms

5. Partial Refunds

  • If a service is partially completed before cancellation, a partial refund may be offered
  • The refund amount will be determined based on the extent of service completion
  • Partial refunds are at the discretion of BorewellMart management
  • Service provider charges for completed work will be deducted from the refund

6. Dispute Resolution

  • If you disagree with a refund decision, you can raise a dispute within 7 days
  • Disputes must be submitted in writing with supporting evidence
  • Our team will review the dispute and respond within 10 business days
  • For unresolved disputes, customers may approach consumer forums

7. Force Majeure

  • In case of unforeseen circumstances (natural disasters, political unrest, etc.), we may need to cancel bookings
  • Full refunds will be provided for force majeure cancellations
  • We will notify customers as early as possible in such situations

8. Payment Gateway Refunds

  • All refunds are processed through Razorpay payment gateway
  • Razorpay's refund policies and timelines apply in addition to our policy
  • Some banks may take additional time to credit refunds to customer accounts
  • We provide refund transaction IDs for tracking purposes

9. Vendor Package Subscriptions

  • Vendor subscription packages are non-refundable once activated
  • Vendors may cancel subscriptions at the end of their billing cycle
  • No prorated refunds for unused subscription periods
  • Special circumstances may be considered on a case-by-case basis

10. Changes to This Policy

BorewellMart reserves the right to modify this Cancellation & Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on our platform. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the modified policy.
